🎵 Thai Tones — simple guide

Thai is tonal: the same sound changes meaning by pitch. Just try your best! 😊

mid 📘
flat, normal voice
maa = come
low 📙
deep, like grumbling
màa = dog
falling 📕
high → drop down
mâa = horse
high 📗
sharp, high pitch
máa = come? (polite)
rising 📒
goes up like question
mǎa = shoulder
💡 Practice: "Mai" can mean new, burn, wood, not?, silk — tone changes everything! Marks like à, â, ǎ, á show tone.
💡 Polite tip: End with khrap (ครับ) male / kha (ค่ะ) female → respect.
